About Us

A bookshop built around the reader, not the algorithm

BookNest exists to make finding the right book effortless and buying it affordable. We began as a single shop in Fort, Mumbai and now ship more than twelve thousand titles across India — fiction, academic sets, business and technology, self-help, children's books, biography and complete competitive-exam libraries.

Every title is hand-checked before dispatch, priced up to 45% below list, and backed by a seven-day replacement promise. Our booksellers answer every question personally — no chatbots, no upsell.
